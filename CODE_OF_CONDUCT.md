# Code of Conduct

This concise policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We aim for a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity/expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity/orientation. Be respectful in issues, reviews, discussions, and when representing Vaultsort elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and considerate of differing perspectives and abilities.
- Give constructive feedback, accept corrections, and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Focus on the project's users and the community rather than personal attacks.
- Respect privacy: never share private personal information, real vault exports, or credentials.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, trolling, sexualized language/imagery, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing others' private information without permission are unacceptable. This applies to public and private interactions within the project's scope.

## Reporting

Use [GitHub's private abuse-reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) for abusive GitHub content; those reports go to GitHub, not automatically to project maintainers. If the maintainer publishes a private contact method on their [profile](https://github.com/Mahmoud217TR), it may be used for project moderation. No dedicated project conduct address is currently documented.

Do not post incident details, private contact information, or sensitive data in public issues to request help. Report technical vulnerabilities using [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not conduct reports.

## Enforcement

The repository maintainer is responsible for project moderation and may edit/remove inappropriate content, reject contributions, or restrict participation. Reports should be handled fairly and with respect for the reporter's privacy.

Following the Contributor Covenant's graduated approach, consequences may include a correction request, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban for serious or repeated violations. Severity and impact guide the response; threats or disclosure of private information can warrant immediate restrictions.

See the [Contributor Covenant FAQ](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/faq/) for background.
